About Our School

Hossana Secondary School is dedicated to providing quality education and helping students develop academically, socially and personally. We encourage discipline, creativity, teamwork and hard work.

To develop responsible and successful learners who contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To provide quality education in a safe, supportive and inspiring environment.

Our Values

Discipline, respect, honesty, teamwork and excellence.
